Every Saudi business with staff on payroll has to navigate the same tangle of labor law, wage-protection reporting, and social-insurance compliance. Ektefa is the platform that handles it for them.

I built and run Ektefa as a SaaS product covering the everyday HR and payroll needs of Saudi businesses — workforce management, scheduling, document management, and employee self-service — integrated directly with the government platforms for wages protection, resident control, and social insurance.

Ektefa now serves around 70,000 active users across the MENA region, generating recurring subscription revenue as one of Zadip Group's six flagship products.
