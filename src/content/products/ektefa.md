Ektefa is a SaaS platform covering the everyday HRM and payroll needs of Saudi businesses — workforce management, scheduling, document management, and an employee self-service application, among other features.

It conforms to local labor regulations and integrates with government platforms for wages protection, resident control, and social insurance, serving around 70,000 users across the MENA region.
