Nafeth launched as the first SaaS product built for car rental offices in Saudi Arabia, introducing electronic rental agreements, one-way and return-anywhere rentals, and a unified customer blacklist to screen out fraudulent or undesired rentees.

The product integrates with state and border-control authorities and has issued over 200,000 contracts since launch.
