Car-rental offices across Saudi Arabia used to run entirely on paper agreements and manual customer vetting. Nafeth was the first SaaS platform built to change that.

I built and run Nafeth as the first SaaS product for car-rental offices in the kingdom — electronic rental agreements, one-way and return-anywhere rentals, and a unified customer blacklist that screens out fraudulent or undesired rentees before they ever reach a counter. The platform integrates directly with state and border-control authorities.

Nafeth has issued over 200,000 contracts since launch, making it the category-defining product in its space.
