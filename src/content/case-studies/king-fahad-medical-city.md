King Fahad Medical City is one of the largest tertiary hospital and research complexes in the Middle East, hosting medical conventions and official visits that demand a presentation standard to match its profile.

I designed and developed a digital, touch-based interactive presentation display system purpose-built for conventions and VIP visits — polished, reliable, and representative of an institution operating at that level.

The system is now part of how the City presents itself to dignitaries, medical professionals, and partners at its highest-profile events.
