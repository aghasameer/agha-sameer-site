Agha conceptualized, designed, and implemented a comprehensive, turnkey volunteer recruitment and management platform for Saudi Arabian Railways — including privately distributed mobile applications and biometric/QR-based attendance systems.

The solution tracks volunteer locations in real time and integrates with crowd-sensors to assess workforce demand, intelligently allocating personnel where they're needed.
