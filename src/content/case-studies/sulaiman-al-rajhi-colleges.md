Sulaiman Al Rajhi Colleges, founded by one of Saudi Arabia's most prominent banking families, is a private university in the Qassim region built with the same standard of institutional rigor as the family's financial enterprises.

I engineered a comprehensive university management portal covering assignment management, submission and grading, academic scheduling, staff services, and — notably — parental access for progress monitoring.

The result is a single platform that serves students, faculty, and families at once — parents get real visibility into academic progress without a single additional phone call to the registrar.
