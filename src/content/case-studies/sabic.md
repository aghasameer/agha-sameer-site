SABIC is one of the world's largest diversified chemical manufacturers — a Fortune Global 500 company headquartered in Saudi Arabia and now part of Saudi Aramco, operating across more than 50 countries.

I designed and implemented a centralized web portal for SABIC's marketing department, built to close out a process that had been running on manual follow-up: tracking invoices from issue to payment across a sprawling, multi-country customer base.

The portal gave the marketing finance team a single system of record for invoicing follow-up — replacing fragmented, manual tracking with one accountable process.
