Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Health oversees the national public hospital network — facilities where a safety system isn't a convenience, it's a condition of operating.

I delivered an Active-RFID based infant-safety and asset-tracking system, built to provide real-time, continuous monitoring in a setting where "real-time" has to mean exactly that.

The system gives hospital staff live location awareness for both infants and critical assets — a category of technology where the only acceptable failure rate is zero.
